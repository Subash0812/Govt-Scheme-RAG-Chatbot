Details

The "Assistance for Purchase of Aids/Appliances" scheme under the Social Welfare, Women & Child
Development Chandigarh financial assistance is being provided to physically handicapped persons for the purchase of aids/appliances to
increase their mobility in their day-to-day working. The applicant should be a resident of UT, Chandigarh to avail of

Benefits

under the
scheme. By providing support for their purchase, the scheme empowers individuals to perform tasks and activities independently,
thereby enhancing their self-esteem and overall well-being.

Benefits

100% cost on purchase of artificial limbs/ aids/ appliances subject to a
maximum of ₹40,000/-

Eligibility

Annual income of the applicant from all sources should not exceed ₹1,50,000/- per annum.Applicant
must be resident of UT, Chandigarh for more than 3 years.Applicant's Disability should be to the extent of 40% and above.Application
